06GOOD TO KNOW

Go well.
Travel easy.

01 When should I visit?＋

October to March is pleasant for most destinations. Monsoon brings dramatic waterfalls and lush hills, while some treks and roads may need extra care.

02 Getting around＋

Bengaluru, Mysuru and Mangaluru connect to many destinations by rail and road. For remote nature spots, check local transport and road conditions before setting out.

03 A little local etiquette＋

At temples and heritage sites, dress respectfully and follow posted photography guidance. Carry water, take your waste with you and leave wildlife undisturbed.
